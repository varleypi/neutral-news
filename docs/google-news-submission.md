# Getting Neutral News into Google News

Two things have to be true. The site has to be *discoverable and legible* to
Google News, which is code, and the publication has to be *claimed* in Publisher
Center, which is a person signing into a Google account. Everything in the first
half is done and in the repo. The second half is four clicks and cannot be
automated — Publisher Center requires an authenticated Google session and puts
new publications through a manual review.

## What is already in place

| Requirement | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| Google News sitemap — last 48 hours, `news:publication`, language, publication date, title | `app/news-sitemap.xml/route.ts` → `/news-sitemap.xml` |
| Both sitemaps declared to crawlers | `app/robots.ts` → `/robots.txt` |
| Full sitemap of every article and archive day | `app/sitemap.ts` → `/sitemap.xml` |
| `NewsArticle` structured data per story, with headline, dates, section and image | `lib/schema.ts`, rendered by `app/article/[slug]/page.tsx` |
| `NewsMediaOrganization` publisher identity, with a 600×60 logo and links to the stated methodology | `lib/schema.ts`, rendered by `app/layout.tsx`; logo at `/logo.png` |
| Stable, descriptive article URLs (`/article/<headline-slug>-<id>`), with permanent redirects from the old numeric ones | `lib/slug.ts`, `app/article/[slug]/page.tsx` |
| One canonical URL per story, including for duplicate coverage | `generateMetadata` in `app/article/[slug]/page.tsx` |
| `og:image` on every page, per-article social cards | `app/og.png`, `app/article/[slug]/og.png` |
| RSS feed and autodiscovery link | `app/rss.xml/route.ts`, `alternatesFor()` in `lib/site.ts` |
| Visible publication date, corrections address and methodology on every article | `components/ArticleDetail.tsx`, `/about` |

Worth knowing before you start: **submission is not what gets a site indexed.**
Google discovers news content algorithmically through the same crawling that
feeds Search, so the work above is what actually determines whether Neutral News
shows up. Publisher Center controls how the publication is *presented* once it
does — the name, the logo, the sections — and is the route into Google News
Showcase.

## The part that needs a person

**1. Verify the site in Google Search Console.**
Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
and add `https://www.neutralnews.us` as a property. Use the DNS or HTML-file
method; if the site is on Vercel, the DNS TXT record is the least fragile.
Do this with the Google account that will own the publication — Publisher Center
verifies ownership automatically when the same account already owns the Search
Console property.

**2. Submit both sitemaps in Search Console.**
Under *Sitemaps*, add:

```
sitemap.xml
news-sitemap.xml
```

The news sitemap will be empty whenever nothing has published in the last 48
hours. That is correct, not a fault — Google drops anything older than two days
from a news sitemap, so listing older articles would only be noise.

**3. Create the publication in Publisher Center.**
Go to [news.google.com/publisher-center](https://news.google.com/publisher-center),
click **Add publication**, and fill in:

- **Publication name:** Neutral News
- **Primary website property URL:** `https://www.neutralnews.us`
- **Location:** United States
- **Primary language:** English
- **Contact email:** piers@spindetector.com
- **Logo:** `https://www.neutralnews.us/logo.png` (600×60, already the logo named
  by the site's structured data — keep the two the same)

Then open **Publication settings → Primary website property URL** and click
**Verify in Search Console**. If step 1 used the same Google account this
completes without further action.

**4. Wait.** New publications are reviewed by hand. Two to four weeks is the
figure Google's own guidance and third-party write-ups both give.

## Where the AI-generated nature of the site matters

Google's news policies do not forbid AI-generated content, but they do treat
content produced primarily to game rankings as spam, and they expect readers to
be told who made what they are reading. Neutral News is already on the right
side of this and should stay there deliberately:

- Every article states in its own footer that it was generated by an AI
  pipeline, names the two reviewers, and gives a corrections address.
- `/about` documents the selection, writing and validation process, and the
  structured data points `publishingPrinciples`, `ethicsPolicy` and
  `correctionsPolicy` at it.

If the review comes back with questions, that page is the answer to most of
them. Do not remove or soften the AI disclosure to improve the odds — an
undisclosed AI publication is the failure mode the policy is actually aimed at.

## Checking the work

```bash
# Both sitemaps parse and carry the right namespaces
curl -s https://www.neutralnews.us/news-sitemap.xml | xmllint --format -
curl -s https://www.neutralnews.us/sitemap.xml | xmllint --format -

# The feed is well-formed
curl -s https://www.neutralnews.us/rss.xml | xmllint --noout - && echo "feed ok"
```

Structured data: paste an article URL into the
[Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results). It should
report an `Article` (Google reads `NewsArticle` as one) plus `Breadcrumbs`, with
no errors.

Social cards: paste an article URL into
[opengraph.xyz](https://www.opengraph.xyz/) or X's card validator and confirm
the 1200×630 card renders with the headline on it.
